Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026. Publisher: Holmes Development Group, Enoree, South Carolina.

Landfall: Hurricane Tracker doesn't collect your personal information.

What stays on your phone

  • Saved places. Names and coordinates you save are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded.
  • Storm briefs (Landfall Pro). A storm brief PDF is made on your device. It includes your saved places' names, their wind chances and, for places near the storm, their spots on the map. It goes only where you choose to share or print it.
  • Site risk spreadsheets (Landfall Pro). An exported CSV is made on your device and includes your saved places' names and coordinates. It goes only where you choose to share it.
  • Map snapshots (Landfall Pro). A shared map image is made on your device. If the "My places" layer is on, it shows nearby saved places' names and their spots on the map. It goes only where you choose to share it.
  • Your location. If you tap "Use my current location," your phone's location is used once, on your device, to save that place. The app doesn't track your location and doesn't send it anywhere.
  • Settings such as units are stored only on your device.

What the app downloads

Public storm data from the National Hurricane Center (nhc.noaa.gov) and from the app's own data service (hosted by Supabase), which republishes processed NHC data. These requests contain no account, name or location. Like any web request, they reveal your device's IP address to the server; the app doesn't use it to identify you, build a profile or sell anything.

Purchases

Landfall Pro is sold and billed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details; it only receives Apple's confirmation that a subscription is active, and that stays on your device.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no sign-in, shows no ads, includes no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app is a general-audience weather app and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting personal data, it will ask first.
