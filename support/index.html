Common questions

The app says there are no storms. Is it broken?

Probably not. When the National Hurricane Center isn't tracking any storms, the Storms tab says so. To see how the app works between storms, tap the gear icon on the Storms tab and turn on Show sample storm. It replays a real past NHC advisory, marked SAMPLE everywhere, and is only available while NHC has no active storms.

Is this an official forecast?

No. The app shows NHC's official forecast first, then forecast models as guidance. Models are not forecasts to act on, and the app never replaces NHC, the National Weather Service or local officials.

Why does the Report Card say PRELIMINARY?

Grades for a storm are checked against NHC's working track until NHC publishes its final Tropical Cyclone Report, usually months after the storm. Until then the numbers can change, so they're labeled PRELIMINARY.

Why isn't the European (Euro) model shown?

Its tracks aren't in NHC's public model files, and showing them requires a separate license. The app only shows data it's allowed to use.

My place alerts didn't arrive.

Place alerts are best effort, not emergency alerts. iOS decides when the app can check in the background, sometimes hours apart, and not at all if you swipe the app away in the app switcher. Keep Wireless Emergency Alerts turned on in your iPhone's settings.

How do I restore Landfall Pro on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app, tap the gear icon, then tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel Landfall Pro?

Open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Landfall Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple handles all billing.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens to my data?

The app has no accounts, ads or tracking, and your saved places stay on your phone. Read the full Privacy Policy.
